# 🚀 Quick Start Guide - Doxa AI Assistant

## Welcome to Your New AI-Powered Community Hub!

---

## 📱 How to Use the AI Chatbot

### Step 1: Navigate to Community Hub
1. Open the Doxa Portal app
2. Click on **"Chat"** in the sidebar
3. Select the **"Community"** tab

### Step 2: Mention the AI
Type your message starting with one of these:
- `@Doxa`
- `@AI`
- `@Bot`
- `@DoxaAI`

### Step 3: Ask Your Question
Examples:
```
@Doxa what does the Bible say about love?
@AI can you help me with a prayer request?
@Doxa I need encouragement today
@AI explain faith to me
```

### Step 4: Wait for Response
- You'll see "🤖 AI typing..." indicator
- Response typically arrives in 2-5 seconds
- AI messages have a purple gradient background with ✨ sparkles

## 🎨 Visual Guide

### Identifying AI Messages

**User Messages:**
- Green background (your messages)
- White background (other users)
- Regular avatar icons

**AI Messages:**
- Purple gradient background
- Bot icon (🤖) in avatar
- Sparkle icon (✨) next to timestamp
- "Doxa AI ✨" name label

### Status Indicators

**When AI is Processing:**
- Header shows: "🤖 AI typing..."
- Send button is disabled
- Your message appears immediately

**When AI Responds:**
- Purple gradient message bubble appears
- Smooth scroll to new message
- Header returns to "Global Family"

## 🆘 Troubleshooting

### AI Not Responding?
1. Check your internet connection
2. Ensure you used @ mention correctly
3. Wait 10 seconds (sometimes takes a bit longer)
4. Try asking again with a simpler question

### Emoji Picker Not Working?
1. Click the smile icon again to close/reopen
2. Refresh the page
3. Try typing emojis directly from keyboard

### Message Not Sending?
1. Check that you're logged in
2. Verify internet connection
3. Try refreshing the page
4. Contact admin if issue persists
